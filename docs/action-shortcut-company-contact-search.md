# Company contact search

The contacts panel on a company detail page now has its own search field.
Search terms are applied together with the existing company-contact relation,
so results stay limited to contacts associated with that company.

Before this shortcut, finding one contact in a large company required paging
through the related list or leaving the company detail and searching the full
Contacts list. The new search keeps the user on the company page and preserves
the entered term while the panel refreshes.

The search is read-only. Clearing the field restores all contacts for the
company, and no contact, company, permission, or external action is changed.
